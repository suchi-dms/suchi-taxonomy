# US nonprofit 501(c)(3)

[HuML file](us-nonprofit-501c3.huml) · [Equivalent TOML](us-nonprofit-501c3.toml) · [Normative reference](../taxonomy-spec.md)

Keep federal status evidence, governance decisions and fundraising records in a tree the next treasurer can understand. Donors and grantmakers remain correspondents; this example does not decide filing duties or guarantee continued exemption.

## Scope and omissions

No determination of exemption, permitted activities, registration jurisdictions or which Form 990 variant is due. Excludes donation valuation, fundraising permission, donor privacy controls and automatic acknowledgment delivery.

All categories have document-kind descriptions. Keywords are literal starter
phrases, not measured classification accuracy; review false matches before use.
No explicit automation seeds: category keywords provide the limited starters.
Sensitivity defaults, review cadences, custom fields, standalone seed families,
links and trust/access state are intentionally absent because taxonomy v1 does
not support them. Configure permissions independently before adding real records.
