# Cross-border and expat household

[HuML file](global-expat.huml) · [Equivalent TOML](global-expat.toml) · [Normative reference](../taxonomy-spec.md)

Keep civil records, residence evidence and work permissions alongside family applications and status decisions. Family members and authorities are correspondents; the tree records evidence without predicting eligibility, deadlines or whether a document is still valid.

## Scope and omissions

Family citizenship/registration applications, dependency evidence and status decisions complete the family outline. No country-specific eligibility test, visa calendar or document-currentness assertion. Apostilles authenticate origin under the applicable procedure, not the correctness of all document contents.

All categories have document-kind descriptions. The tree carries no keywords
or automations; write your own matching rules in Suchi's Automations page and
check what they catch before relying on them. Sensitivity defaults, review
cadences, custom fields, links and trust/access state are left out because
taxonomy v1 doesn't support them. Configure permissions independently before adding real records.
