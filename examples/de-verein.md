# Deutscher Verein

[HuML file](de-verein.huml) · [Equivalent TOML](de-verein.toml) · [Normative reference](../taxonomy-spec.md)

Satzung, Registerunterlagen und Vorstandsentscheidungen bleiben beim Vorstandswechsel auffindbar. Mitgliedsanträge, Beitragsnachweise und Förderunterlagen erhalten eigene Dokumentkategorien; Mitglieder werden nicht zu immer neuen Kategorien.

## Scope and omissions

Membership applications/withdrawals, membership lists/consents and contribution/mandate records complete the membership outline. No automatic dues calculation, consent validity assessment, tax-exempt-status decision or retention/deletion schedule.

All categories have document-kind descriptions. Keywords are literal starter
phrases, not measured classification accuracy; review false matches before use.
No explicit automation seeds: category keywords provide the limited starters.
Sensitivity defaults, review cadences, custom fields, standalone seed families,
links and trust/access state are intentionally absent because taxonomy v1 does
not support them. Configure permissions independently before adding real records.
